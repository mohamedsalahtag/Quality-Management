How to apply it in a new project (same workflow as the email pack)

Copy user-management-pack/ into the new project's repo.
Open a fresh AI session for that project.
Paste INSTALL_PROMPT.md as your first message.
The AI reads SPEC.md, inventories your stack, produces a plan listing schema + LDAP library + auth wiring + routes, then implements after you approve.
