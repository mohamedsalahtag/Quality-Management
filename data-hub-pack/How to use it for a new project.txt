HOW TO USE data-hub-pack FOR A NEW PROJECT
============================================

This pack gives you, in a single bundle, every feature of the Sharbatly QMS
data hub + Perspective Analyzer:

  - A filtered flat-row preview page with an Excel export.
  - A drag-and-drop pivot UI (Rows / Cols / Values / Drill chip zones).
  - Saved perspectives, per user, with manager-shared option + per-user default.
  - Drill-down by cell click or by value picker, scope toggle (page filter
    vs whole dataset), multi-measure, table + Plotly chart renderers,
    polished XLSX export with merged headers and frozen panes.

You do not need to rebuild any of that. Follow these four steps.


STEP 1 — open a fresh AI chat (Claude Code or similar)
------------------------------------------------------

Paste the entire contents of INSTALL_PROMPT.md into the chat. That tells
the AI exactly what to read, what to ask you, and what to produce.


STEP 2 — answer the AI's questions
----------------------------------

It will ask you four things:

  1. What database table(s) you want to pivot over. If you don't have a
     denormalised view yet, the AI will draft one from your tables.
  2. Which dimensions you want to slice by (e.g. Plant, Vendor, Date)
     and which measures (e.g. Sum of Defects, Count of Samples).
  3. What roles in your app can read the analyzer and which can save
     shared perspectives. Defaults are "Supervisor or above" and
     "Manager or SiteAdmin"; you can pick any policy names.
  4. Whether you already have a filter form for the report. If yes,
     paste the filter class. If no, the AI generates a minimal one.


STEP 3 — review the plan and approve
------------------------------------

The AI will produce a written plan listing:

  - The exact files it will copy.
  - The exact lines it will merge into Program.cs and _Layout.cshtml.
  - The migration script for your DB.
  - The view it will create (or update) for the pivot to read from.

Read it. Adjust anything you want. Then say "go".


STEP 4 — verify
---------------

The AI runs through the install. When it's done:

  - Browse to your new report page.
  - Drag two dims to Rows, pick a measure, click Run. Numbers appear.
  - Switch renderer to Bar. A chart appears.
  - Save the perspective, reload the page, load it back. It restores.
  - Click "Export pivot to Excel". A polished XLSX downloads.

That's it. The pack ships everything else.


FILES YOU WILL HAND THE AI
==========================

Just point it at the pack folder. It reads SPEC.md, examples/, and the
reference/ tree on its own.
